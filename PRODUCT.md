# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary audience: AI engineers and software engineers evaluating whether Gustavo's technical judgment is real. They arrive from a blog post, a search, or a link in a thread, and decide in seconds whether the writing is worth their time.
Secondary audience: IT executives (CTOs, heads of engineering, IT directors) deciding whether to bring Gustavo or Ambystech in on AI and software work. They trust the site because engineers would.

## Product Purpose
Personal portfolio and technical blog of Gustavo Barrientos, Azure AI Certified engineer with 15+ years in full-stack, cloud and AI architecture, based in Monterrey, Mexico. It also fronts Ambystech, his software company. Success is two outcomes from one body of work: engineers read and return to the writing, and executives contact him about projects. The writing is the proof for both.

## Positioning
A working engineer's public notebook on shipping AI and .NET systems to production, with the services offer sitting behind that evidence rather than ahead of it.

## Operating Context
Static Astro site on GitHub Pages at https://barrientos.io. Content is Markdown/MDX blog posts, project entries, and `src/data/profile.json`. Posts cover Azure AI, Microsoft Agent Framework, Semantic Kernel, LLM integration, .NET, plus some older hobby and platform posts, some in Spanish.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Voice: one voice, engineer-first. No separate executive site; any leader-facing content uses the same register.
- Primary actions: read the writing; contact Gustavo or Ambystech (`/about#contact`, reCAPTCHA form).
- Existing routes, content collections and schemas stay as documented in CLAUDE.md.
- Static output, no backend.

## Evidence on Hand
- 13 blog posts in `src/content/blog/`, 2 projects in `src/content/projects/`.
- Azure AI Engineer certification, 15+ years of experience (`profile.json`).
- Talks, OSS and other public work: to be supplied by the user. Not yet in the repo.
- Absent, must not be fabricated: client names, case studies, testimonials, metrics, pricing.

## Product Principles
1. The writing is the evidence; everything else points at it.
2. Engineer-first rigor: specific, checkable, no marketing gloss.
3. Executives are served by clarity and credibility, not by a separate pitch.
4. Never claim what the repo cannot show.
